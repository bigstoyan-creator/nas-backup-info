# NAS RustDesk Backup

A personal utility operated by Stoyan Toshev to back up his
self-hosted RustDesk server to his own Google Drive account.

The utility creates password-encrypted archives on the NAS,
uploads them to Google Drive and sends status notifications
to the owner.

This is a private-use application, not a public service or
an official RustDesk product. Backup archives are not hosted
on this website.
